Privacy Policy for the Osela app

Effective date: 08.10.2026

Who we are

The app “Osela — utility bills tracker” (the “App”) is developed by Vladyslav Korolko (developer MixSoft). Privacy contact: vladwin2194@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address, name (if provided) and user ID — for sign-in and syncing across devices.
  • Data you enter: properties, addresses, meter readings, tariffs, charges and payment amounts, notes. Stored in the cloud so it is available on your devices.
  • Device and advertising data: Android advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS version, ad interaction data and crash diagnostics — collected by the Google AdMob SDK.
  • Push notification token — for meter-reading reminders.

How we use data

To provide App features (tracking, calculations, reminders, sync), secure your account, fix bugs and show ads. We do not sell your personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may use the advertising ID and cookies to serve personalised or non-personalised ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google UMP); you can change your choice in the App settings. More: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers that run the App: cloud hosting/authentication and Google (AdMob). We may also disclose data where required by law.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Only the account owner can access their cloud data.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all related data in the App (Settings → Delete account), via korolkoam.github.io/delete-account, or by emailing vladwin2194@gmail.com. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability and to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with its date.
